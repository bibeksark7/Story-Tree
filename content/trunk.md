# Seed trunk

The opening passages of the story, written by hand so the first thing a stranger
reads is reliably good. Everything deeper than this is generated by the AI, which
imitates the voice it finds here — so this file sets the tone for the whole tree.

**Owned by the art/copy half of the team.** See `PARTNER-BRIEF.md`.

## Format

One passage per `##` heading, numbered in order. Under each, the passage itself,
then the two choices that lead out of it. Keep passages to **70–90 words** and
choices to **three to six words**, phrased as actions — that is what the narrator
is instructed to produce, so the trunk has to match or the seam will show.

Do not add extra fields, front-matter, or notes between passages. A script reads
this file by its structure.
