## What this place is

The Lost & Found is a building that does not have an outside. Corridors give
onto rooms that give onto corridors. Things arrive here — objects people set
down somewhere else and did not pick back up. Nobody works here. The building
keeps its own inventory.

## Rules of the world

- Objects that arrive here are real objects. They keep their dents and their
  brand names and their smells. The building does not idealise them.
- The building is indifferent, not malevolent. It is not hunting anyone.
- There is no exit and nobody is looking for one. Visitors are browsing.
- Time is not tracked. Nothing is dated. Seasons do not occur.
- No other people appear on stage. Traces of people do: a receipt, a shoe.

## Voice

Second person, present tense. Plain declarative sentences. Concrete nouns.
No exclamation marks. No rhetorical questions. Do not address the reader as
"dear reader" or acknowledge that this is a story.

The register is a museum label written by someone slightly too invested.

## Vocabulary to prefer

corridor, vestibule, shelf, ledger, hook, crate, drawer, lamp, doorway, sill

## Vocabulary to avoid

magic, portal, realm, destiny, journey, adventure, hero, quest, whisper, ancient
